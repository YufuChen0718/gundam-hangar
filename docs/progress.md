## 2026-11-17 · High-to-low Cycles texture baking for Gundam Guardian
- **Cause:** Decimating `Mesh_0` from `2,002,991` to `30,043` triangles collapsed vertices across UV island seams in the raw Meshy UV layout, causing UVs to interpolate across unrelated texture islands and producing jagged/smeared streaks on the shield star cross, chest vents, red abdomen, and right arm.
- **Method (Bake Settings):** Kept the exact `14,632`-vertex / `30,043`-triangle low-poly geometry in `assets-src/gundam-guardian-source.blend` so all `ASSET_CONFIGS['guardian']` pivots and part boundary planes stay valid. Replaced the collapsed UV layer with a new single-atlas `Smart UV Project` (`66°` angle limit, `correct_aspect=True`, packed with `FRACTION` margin `0.0008` achieving `80.48%` texel coverage across `38,367` unique UVs with zero overlap). Baked from the raw 2M-triangle 8K asset (`assets-src/Meshy_AI_Gundam_Guardian_20261118_texture.blend`) onto the low-poly mesh in Cycles GPU (`METAL` on Apple M4 Pro, `samples = 1`, `Selected to Active`) at `4096x4096` and downscaled ($2\times2$ box filter with tangent-normal renormalization) to `2048x2048`:
  - Two-pass ray casting: tight pass (`cage_extrusion = 0.005 m`, `max_ray_distance = 0.016 m`, `4 px` margin) for overhanging crevices (chest vents, shield border steps) merged with wide fallback pass (`cage_extrusion = 0.015 m`, `max_ray_distance = 0.035 m`, `8 px` margin) for deep decimation chords plus 3-step interior pinhole healing (`0.0` inverted normal fraction).
  - Base color: `DIFFUSE` (`Color` only, no direct/indirect lighting, with high-poly `Metallic=0` and `Normal` unlinked during diffuse pass so metallic/grazing texels never bake dark).
  - Normal: tangent-space `NORMAL` bake (`+X/+Y/+Z`) recovering 2M-triangle panel lines and bevels.
  - Metallic + roughness: baked `Green` (roughness) and `Blue` (metallic) separately via an `Emission` shader (`EMIT`) in `Non-Color` space and packed into one glTF `metallicRoughness` texture (`R=1, G=roughness, B=metallic`).
- **GLB Size & Results:** The `4096` maps total `58.08 MB` (`24.04 MB` color, `20.54 MB` normal, `13.50 MB` MR), which would push the GLB above the 25 MB limit, so we used the unquantized `2048x2048` maps (`6.52 MB` color, `6.42 MB` normal, `4.22 MB` MR; `clean_textures.py` not used). Re-ran `scripts/prepare_mecha.py --asset guardian`: `assets-src/gundam-guardian-source.blend` is **18.70 MB** (`17.83 MiB`) and `public/assets/models/gundam-guardian.glb` is **19.34 MB** (`18.45 MiB`, under 25 MB). Also audited `assets-src/Meshy_AI_Gundam_Guardian_20261118_part-segmentation_split.stl` (added to `.gitignore`; 9 coarse print-bed pieces vs. our 17 in-place articulated parts). Saved high-poly vs. baked low-poly renders (`compare-{front,side,back}.png`), before/after close-ups (`{shield,chest,arm}-{before,after}.png`), and `bake-summary.json` in `docs/results/guardian-bake/`. All 14 unit tests, `npm run build`, and `node scripts/browser-check.mjs` (9-pose seam gap $\le 1.12 \times 10^{-7}\text{ m}$) pass with zero errors.
- **Remaining limitations:** Physical polygon silhouettes at extreme close-up are still bounded by the 30k-triangle decimated edges (even though interior panel lines are sharp via the normal bake), and AI-baked ambient shadows under the rifle/shield/pauldrons in the raw high-poly scan transfer faithfully into the baked albedo.

## 2026-11-15 · T14 Our own Linear Blend Skinning shader & CPU reference (M3)
- Renamed `mecha-right-arm.glb` / `.blend` to `gundam-rigged.glb` / `.blend` across the repository and updated all references.
- Implemented our own Linear Blend Skinning (`src/mecha/lbs.ts`) on plain `THREE.Mesh` objects using a custom `ShaderMaterial` (no `SkinnedMesh`, `Skeleton.update()`, or Three.js skinning shader chunks on the `"lbs"` or `"rigid"` paths) and added a 3-way toggle (**Three.js skinning / our LBS / rigid parts**) in the Inspect panel.
- **LBS Equation & CPU vs. GPU split:**
  - **CPU (per frame):** Our hierarchical FK computes each joint's world transform $W_j$. For each mesh part $m$ (with primary joint bind origin $\mathbf{o}_m$ and precomputed relative inverse bind matrices $\widetilde{B}_{m, j}^{-1} = B_j^{-1} T(\mathbf{o}_m)$), the CPU computes $M_{m, j} = W_{\text{root}}^{-1} W_j \widetilde{B}_{m, j}^{-1}$ and uploads the 15 matrices as `uniform mat4 uBoneMatrices[15]` (`Float32Array(15 * 16)`).
  - **GPU (vertex & fragment shaders):** The vertex shader reads per-vertex `skinIndex` and `skinWeight` attributes and evaluates $\mathbf{p}' = \sum_{k=0}^{3} w_k (M_{m, j_k} (\mathbf{p} - \mathbf{o}_m))$ and $\mathbf{n}' = \mathrm{normalize}\big(\sum_{k=0}^{3} w_k (\mathrm{mat3}(M_{m, j_k}) \mathbf{n})\big)$ (or $\mathbf{p}_{\text{rigid}}' = M_{m, \text{primary}(m)} (\mathbf{p} - \mathbf{o}_m)$ in `rigid parts` mode). The fragment shader samples the original 2K base-color texture (or part debug color) with key/fill directional + hemisphere lighting.
- **CPU LBS Reference & Evaluation (`docs/results/t14-evaluation.md`):** Implemented `LbsManager.cpuPosition` matching the shader math to recompute per-pose browser seam gaps ($4.47 \times 10^{-8}\text{ m}$ to $9.54 \times 10^{-8}\text{ m}$ across the 9 test poses, vs. $103\text{–}236\text{ mm}$ cracks in `rigid parts` mode) and per-vertex differences against Three.js skinning ($1.05 \times 10^{-8}\text{ m}$ to $7.12 \times 10^{-8}\text{ m}$ max diff across 11,694 skinned vertices). Saved shoulder/elbow comparison screenshots (`docs/results/t14-*.png`).
- **Known issues:** Linear Blend Skinning can exhibit slight volume loss ("candy-wrapper" effect) under extreme twisting; dual quaternion skinning is an optional comparison (M9).
